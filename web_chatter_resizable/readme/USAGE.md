- Open a record whose chatter is on the side (large screens).
- Rest the pointer on the left edge of the chatter (the right edge in
  right-to-left languages): a line shows where to grab it.
- Drag the edge to make the chatter wider or narrower, down to its
  standard width.
- Double-click the edge to restore the standard width.
- In form views, click the chatter button next to the pager to hide or
  show the side chatter.

The width and whether the chatter is hidden are kept for the next records
and views of the same kind, in the same browser.
